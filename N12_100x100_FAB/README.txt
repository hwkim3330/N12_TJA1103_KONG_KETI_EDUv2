N12 100x100 mixed fabrication panel

This panel contains the KETI TJA1103 board, the six-key GigaPad board with an external controller/adapter interface, and two MATEnet 2-wire breakouts.

Upload the complete ZIP to the PCB manufacturer. The panel is 100 mm x 100 mm. The GigaPad controller module is intentionally external to avoid physical interference with the six MX switches.
